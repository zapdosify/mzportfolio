# Exhibit Design

## Intro

These projects were done to gain a better understanding in how to design comprehensive project books, work effectively within groups. Organize space, flow and create visual elements for exhibitions and to create visual elements for virtual exhibit design — design graphic solutions within a three dimensional space.

## 1. Geometric Harmony: A Fusion of Digital and Physical Design

Embracing the challenge of transforming conceptual ideas into tangible reality, I embarked on a project that required the fusion of both digital and physical design methods. This venture entailed the creation of a 3D rendering and physical model of three geometric shapes – a sphere, a triangle, and a rectangle – using precise measurements and an array of tools and techniques. The process began with the development of simple models, drawings, and SketchUp files to capture the essence of each shape, ensuring accurate dimensions and proportions that would seamlessly translate into the final physical model.

With the digital groundwork laid, I delved into the physical creation of the model, meticulously crafting each geometric shape using a variety of tools and materials, such as foam, cardboard, and paint. This hands-on approach demanded precision, patience, and a keen eye for detail, as I carefully cut, assembled, and painted each piece to achieve the desired dimensions and aesthetic. The final result – a striking physical model that seamlessly embodies the digital design – stands as a testament to the power of merging traditional craftsmanship with cutting-edge technology, showcasing my ability to bring creative visions to life through a diverse range of design mediums.

## 2. Exhibition Design Prompt 1 — BMX Pop-Up Stand Design (Flipbook)

Embarking on the challenge of creating a pop-up retail space for a BMX bike rental and purchase stand, I envisioned a versatile and transportable solution in the form of a modified shipping container. The concept was inspired by the inherent mobility and adaptability of shipping containers, making them the ideal choice for a retail space that could be easily relocated to remote locations. With the goal of designing an eye-catching and functional space, I set out to develop a comprehensive plan that included both 3D renderings and SketchUp models to illustrate the internal and external layout of the structure.

To begin the design process, I utilized Google SketchUp to create a detailed model of the shipping container. This powerful 3D modeling software allowed me to meticulously plan the interior and exterior layout, maximizing the efficient use of space and ensuring a seamless flow for customer interactions. By incorporating elements such as storage solutions, display areas, and rental stations, I crafted a user-friendly and inviting environment that catered to the unique needs of a BMX bike retail space.

With the SketchUp model complete, I turned to Adobe After Effects to bring the pop-up stand to life through dynamic 3D renderings. These renderings provided a realistic and immersive visualization of the space, showcasing the striking design elements and innovative layout that set this project apart. From the bold use of color and branding to the thoughtful integration of functional elements, the 3D renderings captured the essence of the concept and offered a glimpse into the potential impact of the finished pop-up stand.

## 3. Exhibition Design Prompt 2 — Avengers Museum Exhibit Design (Flipbook)

Creating an immersive museum experience dedicated to the iconic Avengers, I set out to design a captivating space that would not only pay homage to each beloved character but also engage visitors in a unique and memorable journey. Given the constraint of an L-shaped exhibit area, I embraced the opportunity to innovate and optimize the space, dividing the exhibit into distinct segments that would showcase the history, weapons, equipment, background story, contributions, and various props associated with each Avenger. Furthermore, I integrated themed souvenir sections within each segment, offering visitors a chance to bring home a piece of their favorite character's world.

To develop the initial layout and visual concept, I began by sketching and brainstorming ideas, exploring various ways to organize the space while ensuring a seamless flow between segments. I considered factors such as lighting, color schemes, and display techniques that would enhance the storytelling aspect of the exhibit, ultimately crafting a cohesive and visually striking design that would effectively showcase each character's unique attributes.

With a solid design concept in place, I turned to Unreal Engine to bring the Avengers museum to life. This cutting-edge game engine provided the ideal platform for creating an immersive and interactive 3D environment, allowing me to experiment with dynamic lighting, realistic textures, and engaging animations that would draw visitors into the world of each character. Utilizing the powerful tools and features of Unreal Engine, I meticulously crafted each segment, ensuring that every detail accurately represented the essence of the corresponding Avenger while also offering a visually cohesive and engaging experience.

Upon completing the project, I reflected on the unique challenges and opportunities presented by the L-shaped exhibit space and the use of Unreal Engine in designing this immersive museum experience. The final result stands as a testament to the power of innovative design thinking and cutting-edge technology in transforming spatial constraints into opportunities for creative expression. The Avengers museum exhibit showcases not only the fascinating world of these iconic characters but also the boundless potential of immersive design to captivate and inspire visitors, leaving a lasting impression that extends far beyond the exhibit's walls.
