# Magenta Moves

*Category: Internship — T-Mobile*

## Write-up

As an instructional designer at T-Mobile for Business, I embarked on an exciting and unique project - the creation of a "Meeting in a Box" solution aimed at gamifying the learning experience for our sales team. The goal was to provide an engaging, interactive, and fun way for our sellers to increase their skills and knowledge, ultimately driving better performance and results.

The design process started with a deep dive into understanding the needs and preferences of our target audience - the sales team. I conducted interviews and focus groups with team members, as well as collaborating closely with sales managers and subject matter experts. This research phase allowed me to gain valuable insights into the specific challenges faced by the team and the most effective ways to engage them in learning.

With a strong foundation in place, I began to brainstorm ideas for the "Meeting in a Box" concept. I envisioned a portable, easily accessible kit that contained a variety of tools and activities designed to promote learning, collaboration, and skill development. The key was to create an experience that was both fun and educational, fostering an environment in which our sales team would be excited to learn and grow.

To bring this vision to life, I utilized my graphic design skills to create visually appealing and informative materials that would capture the attention and imagination of our sales team. The design elements were carefully crafted to align with T-Mobile's brand identity, ensuring a cohesive and professional look throughout the entire kit.

The "Meeting in a Box" solution included a mix of physical and digital components, such as instructional cards, interactive games, role-playing scenarios, and multimedia presentations. Each component was designed to target specific learning objectives, providing a comprehensive and well-rounded experience for our sellers. For example, a deck of illustrated flashcards was created to help reinforce product knowledge, while an interactive board game encouraged participants to practice their negotiation and problem-solving skills in a fun and engaging way.

To make the "Meeting in a Box" a reality, I worked closely with various vendors to ensure the production of high-quality materials that were both functional and visually appealing. This involved researching and selecting the best suppliers for each component, negotiating contracts and pricing, and overseeing the production process from start to finish.

With the "Meeting in a Box" physically produced, I turned my attention to the implementation and evaluation stages. I collaborated with sales managers to integrate the solution into their regular team meetings, providing them with guidance on how to effectively facilitate the activities and ensure a positive learning experience for all participants.

This project was a unique and innovative approach to sales training that effectively combined instructional design, graphic design, and project management skills. By creating an engaging and interactive learning experience, we were able to equip our sales team with the tools and knowledge they needed to excel in their roles, while also fostering a fun and supportive team culture. it can be used as a testament to the power of creative and out-of-the-box thinking in the world of instructional design.
