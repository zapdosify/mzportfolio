# Symposium Website — Ripple

*Tagline: "Showcasing the next generation of innovative projects." — Masters of Visual Communication and Design*

## Write-up

Designing a website for a symposium is a challenging and exciting process, and the website for Ripple was no exception. This symposium was designed to showcase graduate projects in the form of a hybrid event, and was created in collaboration with different teams responsible for areas such as hybrid symposium management, publicity and fundraising, and publication. The first step in designing the website for Ripple was to understand the goals and objectives of the symposium.

We worked closely within teams to develop a clear understanding of the target audience, the purpose of the event, and the message that we wanted to convey. This information was used to inform the design of the website, ensuring that it aligned with the overall vision of the symposium.

Since Ripple is a hybrid event, we had to design the website with both in-person and virtual attendees in mind. This included designing the website to be responsive across different devices, providing information on how to attend the event in person or online, and creating interactive features such as chat rooms and virtual breakout rooms to promote networking and community-building.

Throughout the design process, we worked collaboratively with different teams to ensure that the website met the needs and expectations of all. This involved regular check-ins and feedback sessions to ensure that everyone was aligned and working towards a common goal. As a result, the website for Ripple is deemed to succeed, attracting attendees from all over the world and showcasing some of the most innovative and impactful graduate projects of the year.

The Ripple project not only allowed me to exercise my creative and technical skills but also served as a valuable lesson in the power of collaboration. By working in unison with diverse teams, I discovered how harmonizing different talents and visions could generate a more cohesive, innovative, and successful design outcome. This experience has instilled in me a deeper appreciation for the importance of open communication, adaptability, and shared passion in achieving collective goals and creating meaningful, memorable design experiences.
