# The Social Pandemic

*Client: — · Year: 2021*

## Topic / Concept

Choosing the topic "The Social Pandemic" is a very important issue that needs to be tackled in the digital age we live in. the looming threat of social media has always existed ever since the platforms became the very window to the outside world. Some may argue why this topic. The question is why not. As much as social media has its positives, there is a need to weigh its cons to establish a healthy balance and understanding of different perspectives, because in a population of 7 billion, not everyone has the same intention to use it in the way it was intended. It is also important to establish that not everyone can cope with it as it is starting to have a severe impact on mental health on the current generation, as well as the future generations to come if it is not tackled. People need to be aware of the significant hold social media has in our everyday lives. Children in the digitalized era are vulnerable and are reaching maturity at an early age by being exposed to social media, they are developing their own perspective of how the world should be. Their own opinions are controlled by the masses because that Is what society considers normal. 'Children have left their childhoods for social media' and that is a powerful strapline to begin with.

## Write-up

As the filmmaker, editor, and motion graphics producer for the documentary "The Social Pandemic," I was responsible for bringing our vision to life and ensuring that the final product was engaging, impactful, and thought-provoking. Throughout the production process, I utilized my skills in filming, editing, and motion graphics to create a high-quality, professional-looking documentary that effectively communicated the message we wanted to convey.

One of the key ways I contributed to the success of this project was through my expertise in filming. I worked closely with my classmates to plan and execute a series of interviews with experts in the field of social media and child protection. I also filmed various b-roll footage to provide visual context and help illustrate the issues discussed in the interviews. My attention to detail and my ability to capture high-quality footage was critical in ensuring that the final product was engaging and visually appealing.

Once we had all of the footage in hand, I took on the role of editor. I worked tirelessly to carefully craft the story we wanted to tell, ensuring that the interviews, b-roll footage, and motion graphics all came together seamlessly. I was able to use my strong editing skills to create a smooth and cohesive narrative, and I was mindful of the pacing and rhythm of the film, so that it held the viewer's attention and effectively conveyed our message.

Finally, I was responsible for the motion graphics production of the documentary. I used my expertise in motion graphics software to create a series of animations and visual elements that helped illustrate the issues we were discussing. From infographics that showed the impact of social media on children's mental health, to motion graphics that helped explain complex concepts, I was able to bring the message of the documentary to life in a visual and engaging way.

Throughout the production process, I was constantly cross-collaborating with my classmates, seeking their feedback and input as I worked to bring our vision to life. I was also able to leverage my strong communication skills to effectively manage the various stakeholders involved in the project, ensuring that everyone was aligned on the end goal and that we were all working towards a common vision.

In the end, I am incredibly proud of the final product that we were able to produce. "The Social Pandemic" is a powerful and thought-provoking documentary that effectively communicates the dangers and risks that children face on social media. I am confident that the film will have a positive impact and will help raise awareness about this important issue.

In conclusion, my skills in filming, editing, and motion graphics production were critical in making this project a success. I am grateful for the opportunity to have worked on such a meaningful and impactful project and I am excited to continue using my skills to create high-quality and engaging content in the future.
