# Animated Shorts

## Write-up

In my quest to push the boundaries of visual design and explore the full potential of the Adobe Suite, I embarked on an ambitious project to merge 2D drawings with 3D environments, creating an immersive and novel visual experience. This innovative endeavor challenged both my creativity and technical prowess, offering a unique opportunity to expand my skill set and develop a captivating new aesthetic. The following stages outline the process of seamlessly blending these two distinct dimensions, highlighting the specific Adobe tools and techniques employed along the way.

**1. Concept Development and Sketching**

The project began with brainstorming and conceptualization, as I sought to envision the ideal fusion of 2D and 3D elements. I contemplated various themes, compositions, and visual styles, ultimately settling on a concept that combined traditional hand-drawn illustrations with a dynamic, digitally-rendered environment. With the concept in mind, I sketched out a series of rough drafts, experimenting with different compositions and perspectives to ensure the optimal integration of 2D and 3D components.

**2. Adobe Illustrator: Refining 2D Illustrations**

Using Adobe Illustrator, I meticulously transformed my initial sketches into clean, crisp vector illustrations. This powerful vector editing tool allowed me to fine-tune the details of each 2D element, creating a consistent and cohesive visual style. Additionally, Illustrator's extensive features and precise control over strokes, fills, and gradients enabled me to imbue the 2D drawings with depth and dimensionality, preparing them for seamless integration into the 3D environment.

**3. Adobe After Effects: Building the 3D Environment**

The next phase involved constructing a detailed 3D environment using Adobe After Effects, a cutting-edge tool for creating realistic 3D scenes and compositions with the help of the Element 3D plugin. I experimented with various shapes, materials, and lighting conditions to craft a visually compelling and harmonious space that would perfectly complement the 2D illustrations. Dimension's intuitive interface and powerful rendering capabilities allowed me to bring my vision to life.
